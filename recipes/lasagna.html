Homemade Lasagna

Description:

Homemade lasagna that's a family favorite and a great way to feed a crowd!

Ingredients:

  • Lasagna pasta
  • Beef and sausage
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Pasta sauce
  • Spices
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Egg
  • Tomato paste
  • Mozzarella
  • Parmesan
  • Parsley

Steps:

  1. Boil pasta: In a large pot of salted water boil lasagna noodles according to the instructions.
  2. Prepare meat sauce: Cook sausage and beef with onion and garlic. Drain well, add the pasta sauce and simmer for a few minutes to thicken.
  3. Combine cheese mixture: Stir the cheese mixture together in a bowl
  4. Layer and back: Layer the meat sauce and cheese mixture with lasagna noodles and bake until the top of the lasagna is golden brown.
